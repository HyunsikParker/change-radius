# Change Radius privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Change Radius is a Forge app that runs on Atlassian's platform inside your Jira and Confluence site.

## What the app reads
When someone opens the Change impact panel, the Release impact page or the Rovo agent, the app reads, as that person
and with that person's permissions: Jira issues that carry a `trace-` label in the current project (summary, labels,
status, fix versions and issue links), the remote links of those issues, the project's versions, and the Confluence
space you name when you create an impact record.

## What the app writes
Only when a person asks for it: sample issues and links (Load sample trace), an issue link (Link on a suggestion), a
Confluence page and a remote link to it (Create impact record), and re-verification tasks with their links. Everything
it writes stays in your Atlassian site.

## What the app stores
Nothing. The app has no database or storage of its own and sends no data outside Atlassian. The developer does not
collect, receive or sell personal data. Atlassian's platform keeps its usual operational logs for Forge apps; see
Atlassian's privacy policy.

## Contact
Questions and requests: the issues page of the app's public code repository.
